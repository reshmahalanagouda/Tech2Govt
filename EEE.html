Electrical & Electronics Engineer (EEE)

We are seeking a dedicated Electrical and Electronics Engineer to join our engineering department. You will be responsible for designing, maintaining, and supervising large-scale electrical systems and electronic components that support critical public infrastructure. The role demands a commitment to safety standards and the ability to manage complex projects from conception to completion.

Key Responsibilities

  1. 1. System Design & Implementation: Design and develop electrical products, circuits, and systems using CAD software like AutoCAD or MATLAB.

  2. 2. Infrastructure Maintenance: Oversee the operation and maintenance of power generation, transmission, and distribution networks.

  3. 3. Project Supervision: Manage government projects, ensuring they meet technical specifications, timelines, and budgets.

  4. 4. Safety & Compliance: Conduct risk assessments and ensure all installations comply with national electrical codes (e.g., NEC, IEEE) and safety regulations.

  5. 5. Technical Testing: Perform detailed calculations and testing on electrical components to confirm their reliability and efficiency.

  6. 6. Reporting: Prepare technical documentation, progress reports, and budget estimates for senior officials.

Exam Details:

While GATE is not strictly a government exam, it is often a prerequisite for securing a job in public sector undertakings (PSUs). The exam tests the detailed understanding of various undergraduate subjects in engineering and is used by many PSUs to hire candidates for positions in their organisations.

GATE is conducted to admit students for master’s degrees at top IITs and premier colleges and to select qualified candidates for Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) jobs.

Exam Pattern:

The GATE exam consists of multiple choice questions (MCQs) and numerical answer questions from subjects like Mathematics, Engineering, and General Aptitude. It is conducted in online mode.

Exam/Pay Details:

GATE is conducted to admit students for master’s degrees at top IITs and premier colleges and to select qualified candidates for Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) jobs.


  1. Approx No. of vacancies - 200+ PSU jobs
  2. Recruiters - ~30 (IOCL, ONGC, BHEL, etc)
  3. Avg No. of Applicants - 8 to 10 lakhs
  4. Salary - over INR 40,000 to 1.4 lakhs per month
  5. Basic Eligibility - Engineering degree

Key Eligibility Criteria:


GATE exam eligibility requires candidates to hold a bachelor's degree in engineering, technology, architecture, science, commerce, or arts, or be in their 3rd or higher year of such a program.


  1. 1. Academic Qualification: Candidates must have completed or be in the final/pre-final year (3rd year or higher) of a Bachelor's degree (\(>3\) years) in Engineering/Technology/Architecture/Science/Arts/Commerce.


  2. 2. Degrees Accepted: B.E., B.Tech., B.Arch., B.Pharm., B.Sc., M.Sc., M.A., MCA, or equivalent.


  3. 3. Professional Society Certificates: Candidates with certificates from professional societies (like AMIE) recognized by MoE/AICTE/UGC as equivalent to B.E./B.Tech. are eligible.


  4. 4. Age Limit: There is no age limit for taking the GATE exam.


  5. 5. Minimum Marks: While no strict minimum percentage is usually mentioned in general guidelines, some specific courses/institutes may require a minimum percentage (typically 55-60%).


  6. 6. Nationality: Indian nationals and candidates from Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Singapore, Ethiopia, and UAE are eligible.
