Exam Details:

The government exam patterns for Computer Science (CS), Information Science (IS), and Data Science (DS) engineers differ markedly from traditional branches like Civil or Mechanical.

Instead of focusing heavily on junior engineer (JE) positions, the recruitment landscape for CS/IS/DS fields leans towards Scientist, Programming, Analyst, and R&D Class-A Officer roles across major central boards.

Exam Pattern:

1. Direct PSU & Research Entry via GATE

Most Maharatna and Navratna Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) use the GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) as their primary screening mechanism. Data Science (DS) applicants take the GATE DA paper, while CS/IS applicants take the GATE CS paper.


  1. Total Marks: 100 Marks | Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
  2. Question Layout: 65 Questions categorized into three formats:
    1. MCQs: Standard Multiple Choice Questions (Features 1/3 or 2/3 negative marking).
    2. MSQs: Multiple Select Questions (More than one option can be correct; no negative marking, no partial credit).
    3. NATs: Numerical Answer Type (Fill-in-the-blank mathematical calculations; no negative marking).
  3. Paper Breakdown:
    1. General Aptitude: 15 Marks (Verbal and quantitative logic).
    2. Core Technical Domain: 85 Marks (CS/IS or Data Science syllabus details).
2. NIELIT / NIC Scientist-B Exam Pattern

TThe National Informatics Centre (NIC) and National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology (NIELIT) recruit Class-A Technical Officers using a standalone, objective examination format.


  1. Total Questions: 120 Questions | Duration: 3 Hours
  2. Exam Delivery: Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) / OMR Option.
  3. Structure:
    1. Section A (Technical Area - 65%): 78 Questions covering Data Structures, Algorithms, DBMS, Operating Systems, Web Technologies, and Cloud Computing.
    2. Section B (Generic Area - 35%): 42 Questions checking Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Analytics.
  4. Marking Criteria: +1 mark for a correct answer; 0.25 marks deducted for each error.
  5. Final Phase: Written clearing leads to a Technical Interview panel.
  6. 3. ISRO Scientist / Engineer (SC) Exam Pattern

    7. The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) conducts its own centralized recruitment drive (ICRB) for CS/IS graduates to work on mission telemetry, software systems, and data infrastructure.

    1. Total Marks: 100 Marks | Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
    2. Test Segments:
      1. Part A (Technical): 80 MCQs evaluating core engineering subjects (Computer Architecture, Networks, OS, Compiler Design). Includes a 1/3 negative marking deduction.
      2. Part B (Aptitude): 15 to 20 MCQs covering spatial, logical, and numerical abstraction. No negative marking in this section.

Key Eligibility Criteria:


The eligibility criteria for government Computer Science (CS), Information Science (IS), and Data Science / Analytics (DA) engineering jobs lean heavily toward Class-A Scientist, Programming, and R&D Officer roles, alongside specialized IT Officer tracks in banking and public sectors.


  1. 1. Educational Qualifications


    2. Your eligibility pathway depends on your exact technical degree, as some specialized boards have precise branch requirements:


  2. For B.E. / B.Tech / M.Tech Graduates (CS / IS / DA)
    1. - Eligible Branches: Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), Information Science & Engineering (ISE), Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI-ML).
    2. - Eligible Posts: Scientist-B (NIC/NIELIT), Scientist/Engineer-SC (ISRO), Scientific Officer (BARC/DRDO), IT Officer (IBPS SO/SBI), and System Analyst (State PSCs).
    3. - Cross-Eligibility Details:
      1. GATE CS Paper: Accepts CS, IT, and IS students for almost all traditional PSU roles (like POSOCO, NTPC, and IOCL).
      2. GATE DA Paper: Accepts CS, Data Science, and core mathematics/statistics backgrounds for analytical roles in technical organizations.
    4. - Alternative Degrees: Many exams (like NIELIT or IBPS SO) consider MCA (Master of Computer Applications) or an M.Sc in Computer Science/IT as completely equivalent to a B.Tech degree.

    For 3-Year Diploma Holders (CS / IT)

    1. Eligible Posts: Junior Engineer (IT), Technical Assistant, and System Administrator.
    2. Key Limitations: Diploma holders are restricted to entry-level executive roles like RRB JE (IT) or state-level technical assistant positions. They are not eligible for high-tier Scientist positions at ISRO, DRDO, or NIC.

  3. 2. Age Limits (As of 2026 Regulations)


    4. The standard age brackets for General / Unreserved category candidates across the major ECE recruiting bodies are:


    Exam Category / Board Minimum Age Maximum Age (General)
    NIELIT / NIC (Scientist & Technical Assistant) 18 / 21 Years 30 Years
    ISRO / BARC / DRDO (Scientist Posts) 18 / 21 Years 28 to 30 Years (varies by scientist rank)
    RRB JE / SSE (Indian Railways) 18 Years 33 Years (SSE has higher cap)
    IBPS SO / SBI SO (Bank IT Officers) 20 Years 30 Years (highly state-dependent)
    State PSCs / State IT Departments 21 Years 35 to 40 Years (highly state-dependent)
    PSUs via GATE (NTPC, ONGC, PowerGrid) 21 Years 28 to 30 Years

  4. 3. Government Age Relaxations


    5. Upper age limits are extended for reserved categories across all central and state exams:


    1. - OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 Years relaxation
    2. - SC / ST: 5 Years relaxation
    3. - PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities): 10 to 15 Years relaxation

    4. Minimum Percentage / CGPA Requirements


    Unlike general government exams (which just require a pass), top-tier technical boards enforce strict academic cutoffs:

    1. - ISRO & BARC: Strictly require a minimum of 65% aggregate marks or a CGPA of 6.84/10 throughout your B.E./B.Tech.
    2. - PSUs via GATE: Most Maharatnas (like NTPC, IOCL, and PowerGrid) require a minimum of 60% marks in graduation.
    3. - UPSC ESE & RRB JE: Require a simple Pass Class degree or diploma with no specific minimum percentage restriction.
    4. - NIELIT / NIC: Requires a simple First Class degree (minimum 60% marks or equivalent CGPA).
    5. - Banking Sector (IBPS SO): Requires a simple Pass Class degree from a recognized university, with no strict minimum percentage bracket.
